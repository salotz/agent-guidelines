# Summary: Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF)

Source: https://aaif.io/

## Overview
AAIF focuses on operationalizing agentic AI for enterprise scale through standards, governance, and community projects.

Key areas include working groups on:
- Accuracy & Reliability
- Agentic Commerce
- Governance, Risk & Regulatory Alignment
- Identity & Trust
- Observability & Traceability
- Security & Privacy
- Workflows & Process Integration
- Taxonomy & Landscape

Provides resources, events, and projects for building trustworthy autonomous agent systems.

Use as a reference for standards and best practices in agentic systems when relevant.
